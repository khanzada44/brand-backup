## Edit prompt

Edit this exact attached American Authors logo, do not redesign it. Remove the entire white background and all excessive whitespace. Preserve the exact eagle head and spread wings merging into an open book with two red page strokes on each side, the single gold five-point star centered above it, and the exact serif wordmark 'American Authors' below. Preserve the original shapes, proportions, spelling, typeface and composition; no new elements or altered layout. Deliver a tightly framed horizontal logo with a small even margin and genuine transparent alpha, including transparent holes and page separations. No background rectangle, checkerboard, shadow, glow, texture or white halo.

Header variant prompt: retain the original navy eagle and word American, red book strokes and word Authors, and gold star for light backgrounds.

Footer variant prompt: replace navy eagle shapes and word American with ivory white, use brighter warm red for book strokes and word Authors, and retain the gold star. Keep the open negative space inside the eagle head transparent for dark backgrounds.
