# Journal image assets

Created November 5, 2026 with the built-in image generation tool. Both images were generated from new prompts without reference images. WebP variants are stored in assets/images/journal/ for listing cards, article banners, thumbnails and supporting sections.

## Writing image prompt

Create a wide landscape editorial photograph for an American Authors journal article about developing a book from first draft to publication. A real working writer's desk in a quiet sunlit studio: a neatly gathered stack of manuscript sheets with indistinct text, an open cream notebook, a dark fountain pen, a closed plain navy clothbound book, a small cup of coffee. Thoughtfully used materials, natural paper grain and soft side light, warm ivory, walnut and muted navy palette, calm and credible, contemporary literary magazine photography. Wide horizontal composition with the objects grouped naturally in the centre so it crops well to a panoramic article banner or square thumbnail. Photorealistic, understated, no people, no readable text, no lettering, no logos, no watermark. High quality finished website asset, not a website mockup.

Files: writing-hero.webp, writing-card.webp, writing-thumb.webp, writing-spread.webp and writing-detail.webp.

## Promotion image prompt

Create a wide landscape editorial still life photograph for an American Authors journal article about introducing a new book to readers. A plain deep teal paperback book standing on a warm oak tabletop beside a small stack of matching copies, a few blank cream launch postcards and bookmarks, a closed laptop and an author's notebook softly out of focus behind them, a subtle sprig in a simple ceramic vase at the far edge. A quiet real publishing studio preparing a book release, soft morning window light, warm ivory and restrained deep teal and navy colours, tactile paper and cloth, premium literary magazine photography, credible everyday scene. Wide horizontal composition with book and campaign material central and room for panoramic and thumbnail crops. No people, no readable text, no logos, no book cover lettering, no watermark. Photorealistic finished image, not an infographic or website mockup.

Files: promotion-hero.webp, promotion-thumb.webp and promotion-cta.webp.
